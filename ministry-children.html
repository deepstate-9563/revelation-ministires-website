Children Ministry

Welcome to Our Little Explorers' Corner!

Hello, Parents and Young Adventurers

We’re delighted to introduce you to our Children’s Ministry, a place where the wonder-filled journey of faith begins.
Our ministry is like a treasure chest of discovery, where your little explorers will find joy, friendship, and the loving embrace of God’s teachings.

Our Mission:

At Our Little Explorers’ Corner, we embark on a marvelous expedition of faith. Our mission is simple yet profound: to make every moment a special opportunity for children to connect with God, learn about the Bible, and grow in kindness and love.

Join us at Revelation Wisdom Ministries and embark on a life-changing journey towards liberation, enlightenment, and empowerment. Together, let us unlock the fullness of your potential and bring glory to God through the fulfillment of your pre-ordained purpose.

Sporting Activities

Let's Get Active and Have Fun!

If your children have a passion for sports or simply want to have some fun, they’re welcome to participate in our sporting activities. Let’s cheer for your child’s physical and spiritual growth!

we’re thrilled to offer your young adventurers the chance to get active and play together through various sporting activities. We believe that physical activity is a fantastic way for children to learn teamwork, discipline, and have a blast while doing it.

Dining in Unity

Strengthening Bonds through Communal Meals

our church emphasizes values and virtues among children during communal group eating. These gatherings teach our young ones the importance of sharing, gratitude, inclusivity, respect, and teamwork.

As we dine in unity, we also aim to build unity of character, ensuring that these values and virtues become an integral part of their identities, fostering a loving and inclusive community where everyone is embraced as family.

We are an apostolic and prophetic ministry mandated to liberate, enlighten and equip you, empowering you to effectively fulfill your pre-ordained purpose in life to God’s Glory
